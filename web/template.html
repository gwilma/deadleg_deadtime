Water Hammer Check

Will this hot water pipe need a surge arrester? Enter the run, the flow and the valve that closes it. The page simulates the pressure surge and checks it against the 2 bar limit.

Pipe
Run and flow

Measure the length from the valve back to the nearest point that holds the pressure steady: a manifold, a much larger pipe, the cylinder or an expansion vessel.

Valve

Limits

2 bar is the rise allowed by EN 806-2 and DIN 1988-200 (VDI 6006). Use the lowest rating of the pipe and fittings at the water temperature.

Ways to fix it

    Method. Wave speed from the Korteweg formula for a thick-walled pipe. The surge is the larger of the closed-form estimate (Joukowsky ρav if the valve closes within the critical time 2L/a, otherwise Michaud 2ρLv/t) and a method-of-characteristics simulation of the pipe with steady friction and an orifice valve whose opening closes at a steady rate. Water properties from IAPWS-95; plastic moduli are indicative short-term values.

    Sources. 2 bar surge limit: EN 806-2 and DIN 1988-200 as quoted in VDI 6006:2017 §4.3. Peak pressure: Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999, Schedule 2. Advisory velocity: Uniform Plumbing Code 610.12. Valve closure times are engineering estimates; measure the valve if a result is marginal.
